## Known open (not from this deploy)

**Correction.** An earlier draft of this entry claimed Outlook never parses
`.ics` attachments and that Gmail's handling was broken. Both claims were wrong
and are retracted. Bucketing the ICS-bearing emails by ingest date shows a clean
cutover rather than a defect:

| Ingested | ICS emails | Components |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-28 | 6 | **6 (100%)** |
| before 2026-08-22 | 18 | 0 |

Calendar-component capture (migration `20260819000001`) reached production in
the 2026-08-22 cutover. Every ICS email ingested after that produced a
component. The 18 misses all predate the deploy and were never backfilled.

What remains genuinely open is not parsing but **matching reach**:

- Only ~4% of events carry a strong identity hint (14 hints / 364 events),
  because only recent mail has components and most event mail carries no `.ics`
  at all.
- Without a hint, the candidate set is **the local day of the extracted start**
  (`find_matching_event`: `gte(start_datetime, local_day) lt(local_day + 1d)`).
  An event rescheduled to a different date is therefore outside its own
  candidate window and cannot be recognised as the same event — it becomes a new
  event rather than a change proposal.
- Same-day duplicates from different emails are resolved by an LLM text
  comparison, which declines on divergent titles. Two live examples on 2026-09-18:
  "Ice Cream Social & Costume Swap" and "Ice Cream Social & PTA Meeting".

Fixing this is a feature change to the resolution ladder (a hint that survives a
date change, and identity-based matching against existing calendar entries), not
a bug fix. It is not attempted here.
